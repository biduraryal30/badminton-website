My Opinion on Badminton

Badminton is more than just a racquet sport; it's a dynamic chess match played at lightning speed! In Nepal, badminton has seen massive popularity across all age groups because of its accessibility and health benefits.

I believe that with better indoor stadium infrastructure and structured grassroot coaching programs, Nepal has immense potential to raise national champions capable of competing internationally. The agility, mental sharpness, and fast reflexes required make it one of the most exciting games in the world!

Basic Rules of Badminton

Scoring System

  • A match consists of the best of 3 games of 21 points each.
  • Every time there is a serve, a point is scored (Rally scoring).
  • At 20-all, the side which gains a 2-point lead first wins that game.

Service Guidelines

  • Service must be struck underhand below waist level (1.15m height).
  • Serve diagonally into your opponent's service court.
  • If score is even: serve from right court. If odd: serve from left court.

Faults & Violations

  • Shuttle landing outside boundaries or hitting the ceiling/net.
  • Player touches the net with their body or racquet during play.
  • Double-hitting the shuttle on the same side of the court.

Court Boundaries

  • Singles: Long and narrow boundaries apply.
  • Doubles: Short and wide service court during serve, full court after serve.
